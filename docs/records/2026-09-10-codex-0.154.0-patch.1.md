## Porting Notes

Upstream `rust-v0.154.0` independently converged on parts of two of our patches:

- `TurnStartParams`/`TurnSteerParams` gained a native
  `client_user_message_id: Option<String>`, and `AppCommand::UserTurn` gained an
  identical `client_id`-equivalent `String` field, matching what
  `editable-enter-queue` also adds. Reused upstream's fields instead of
  duplicating them.
- Upstream refactored their own pending-steer tracking from parallel `VecDeque`s
  into one `PendingSteer { client_id, user_message, history_record, compare_key }`
  struct -- independently convergent with our own parallel-array-to-struct design
  for the same subsystem, but for a different purpose (upstream: retry/dedup;
  ours: recall-for-edit via `TurnSteerRecallStatus`). Merged our
  `active_turn_id`/`edit_order`/`state` extensions onto upstream's consolidated
  struct rather than keeping two parallel data models.

`tui-notification-sound` and `plan-mode-default` also carried a pre-existing gap,
unrelated to this upstream bump: neither ever updated
`core/config.schema.json` for their new config fields.
`config_schema_matches_fixture` would have failed identically against the old
0.153.4 base -- it was never run because `nix flake check`'s `doCheck` is empty.
Fixed via `just write-config-schema`, folded into the `plan-mode-default` port
commit.
